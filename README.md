# Unicore

A web platform that gathers university schedules in one place and gives each student a personal, organized timetable. Starting with Benha University, Faculty of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, and designed to expand to other faculties and universities.

## Features
- Personal schedule by university, faculty, year, group, and section
- Notifications before lectures and sections
- Faculty guide for halls, rooms, and labs
- Search for doctors and TAs
- Exam schedule and announcements
